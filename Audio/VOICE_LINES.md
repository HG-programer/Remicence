## Recording Notes
- Use a gentle, caring tone
- Speak slowly and clearly
- Add slight pauses for natural rhythm
- Consider adding soft background music or ambient sounds
- Keep recordings between 2-5 seconds for most lines
- Use consistent audio levels across all files

## TTS Configuration
If using Text-to-Speech instead of recorded audio:
- Voice: Female, gentle tone
- Speed: Slightly slower than normal
- Pitch: Warm, mid-range
- Emotional tone: Caring, supportive
- Language: English (with option for other languages)
